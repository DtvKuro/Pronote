## INFORMATION ENGINE

A computer is electronic device that accepts data, processes it according to instruction, produces info and stores the result.
Anything that can be input into the computer is Information, even as simple as typing name in it.

RAW DATA ---> PROCESS ---> INFORMATION (Processed data).
